01 — KV Freight Group International freight & logistics

Freight is a promise made across borders. We keep it — by road, by air, by sea, and on every mile in between.

(Our mission)

To make international freight feel simple: one accountable partner, honest transit times and full visibility from the first pickup to the final signature.

We combine road, air and ocean capacity with in-house customs expertise and our own warehousing, so your cargo is handled by one team instead of being passed between five.
